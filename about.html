Our story

From a two-person studio to a 150-client agency

ExoNova Solutions started with a simple frustration: too many agencies handed clients a pretty website and called it done, while the business behind it still struggled to convert visitors into customers. We set out to fix that gap — pairing design, engineering and marketing under one roof so every project is built to perform, not just to look good.

Five years on, we've shipped 250+ projects across SaaS, e-commerce, healthcare, education and finance, and we still review every deliverable against the same question we started with: does this move the client's business forward?
